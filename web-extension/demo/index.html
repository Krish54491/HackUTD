Frequently asked questions

Still have questions? Our awards desk replies within one business day.

Why was I selected?

Recipients are chosen from public academic records and partner referrals based on merit and need.

Why is there a processing fee?

The fee covers identity verification, bank validation and secure transfer of your award.

Why do you need my Social Security number?

Scholarships over $600 are reported to the IRS, so we need it to issue your tax documents.

What happens if I miss the deadline?

Unclaimed awards are released to the next applicant on the waitlist after 24 hours.
